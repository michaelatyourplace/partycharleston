Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Charleston Party Hub ("us", "we", or "our") operates the website at partycharleston.com (the "Service"), a guide to bachelorette parties, nightlife, bar crawls and group celebrations in Charleston, South Carolina. This page explains what information is collected when you use the Service, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The Short Version

  • We don't have accounts, contact forms or newsletter sign-ups, and we don't ask for your name or email.
  • Basic, anonymized traffic data may be used to see which pages people read.
  • Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click one, the retailer may set its own cookies.
  • Links to venues, booking sites and shops take you to sites we don't control.
  • We don't sell personal information.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information from our visitors. Our website is for informational purposes only. We do not have user accounts, contact forms, or newsletter sign-ups.

Some pages include a "Share with the group" button. It uses your device's built-in share menu or copies the page link to your clipboard. Nothing you share passes through our servers.

2. Log Data

We may collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Service ("Log Data"). This Log Data may include information such as your computer's Internet Protocol ("IP") address (anonymized), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, the time spent on those pages, and other statistics. This data is used for website traffic analysis and is not used to personally identify you.

3. Cookies

We do not use cookies to track user activity on partycharleston.com. Cookies are files with a small amount of data, which may include an anonymous unique identifier. Since we do not set them, no personal information is stored via this method on our site.

Third-party sites you visit from our links, including retailers reached through affiliate links, may set their own cookies once you arrive there. Those cookies are governed by that site's privacy policy, not this one.

4. Third-Party Services Used to Run the Site

To load quickly and look consistent, our pages pull some files from outside providers. When your browser loads a page, it connects directly to these providers, which can see your IP address and basic browser details as part of delivering the file:

  • jsDelivr, which serves the Bootstrap framework and icon files
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts used on the site
  • Unsplash, which hosts some background photos

We don't receive any personal information from these providers. Each has its own privacy policy.

5. Affiliate Links and Disclosure

Some pages, such as our bachelorette decor and party game picks, contain affiliate links. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Pages with affiliate links say so next to the link, and those links are marked "sponsored" in the code.

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

Affiliate programs track a click so the retailer knows it came from us. That tracking happens on the retailer's site, not ours, and we never see your name, address or what else you buy.

Our experience listings for venues, tours and local businesses are independent. Businesses don't pay to be listed or write their own listings.

6. Links to Other Sites

Our Service may contain links to other sites that are not operated by us, including venue websites, booking platforms such as OpenTable, and online shops. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that third party's site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

If you book through a third-party link, any information you enter, such as your name, phone number or payment details, goes directly to that business or platform.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is written for adults planning celebrations and nightlife in Charleston, and some content covers bars and age-restricted venues. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Because we don't collect personal information through accounts or forms, there is generally no personal profile for us to access, correct or delete. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

You can limit what third parties see by blocking or clearing cookies in your browser settings, using a content or tracker blocker, or browsing in a private window. The site works normally with cookies turned off.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at YOUR-EMAIL-HERE.
