Good to Know

Girls Night Out Charleston
Details

What Girls Night Out Is

Girls Night Out is a private live show in Charleston, SC, with Michael as the performer. It's crowd-interactive, with music, dancing, games and comedy, held at your rental or venue. It costs $200 for up to 90 minutes. Think live show meets celebration, not a bar night.

How to Book Michael

Call or text 843-360-9438, or visit michaelatyourplace.com. No forms, and you pay on arrival. Last-minute dates are welcome, but weekends from March through October fill fast.

Where the Show Happens

At your location: an Airbnb, vacation rental, private home or event space anywhere in the Charleston area, including downtown Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Isle of Palms, Folly Beach and Sullivan's Island.

Group Size

The show works for groups from 8 to 40 guests, and 10–20 is the sweet spot for the most interaction. For bigger groups, call or text ahead to plan the setup.

What Pairs Well With the Show

It works best as the evening's centerpiece. Pair it with dinner beforehand at Church and Union or The Ordinary, then bars like Prohibition or Uptown Social after. For full-day plans, add the spa at Charleston Place, a harbor party boat or crafting with mimosas.

Ages

The show is for adults 18 and over. Mixed-age friend groups, moms celebrating birthdays, college sorority nights, divorce parties and girls trips are all welcome. Under 21? See college nightlife in Charleston.
