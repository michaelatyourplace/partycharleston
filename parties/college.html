🍹 College Entertainment Weekends

Under 21 doesn't mean staying in. These College of Charleston-area venues let the 18+ crowd in for shows, dancing and party nights.

Music Farm

📍 32 Ann Street

Charleston's main live music venue and the go-to for the 18-20 crowd. Check the calendar for touring DJs, rock bands, hip-hop and EDM acts. Tickets typically run $20-50.

18+ shows

Honky Tonk Saloon

📍 192 College Park Rd, Ladson

A line-dancing hangout outside downtown, and a solid dance spot for the college crowd. Check the calendar for DJs and line-dancing nights, and plan a ride for the drive.

18+ shows

Dudley's

📍 42 Ann Street

Join the LGBTQ+ community for dancing, drag or one of their party nights, a few doors down from Music Farm. 18 to enter, 21 to drink.

18+ to enter

🏖️ Daytime, Beach & Big-Event Plans

The best college weekends start before dark. Grab a car, split gas and head for the water.

🌊

Beach Day at Folly

Folly Beach is the classic student beach: the pier, surf, and Center Street for food after. It's about 20 minutes from campus, so go early on weekends before parking fills.

🏰

Sullivan's Island

A quieter beach day with Fort Moultrie on the same trip. Good for a smaller group.

🦐

Red's Ice House

Waterfront food on Shem Creek in Mount Pleasant. Go for sunset. Site

🦪

Bowens Island

Casual, no-frills seafood on James Island, a local favorite on the way back from Folly. Site

🎶

The Windjammer

The Isle of Palms beach bar, with music on the sand. Check ages for each show. Site

Big events

Building 64

The Navy Yard event campus in North Charleston has hosted big parties for the last decade, including Kulture Klash, Wine and Food Party@the Powerhouse and the High Water after party. Check ages on each event before you buy.

College of Charleston Is a Party School, Kind Of

Here's what college nightlife looks like for CofC students.

The Real CofC Experience: House Parties

Let's be honest: house parties are what most students actually do on any given weekend. They're where freshmen become friends, where upperclassmen hold court on front porches, and where the real social fabric of CofC gets woven together.

College of Charleston nightlife is what earns CofC its party school reputation. There are no College of Charleston bars. The campus just happens to sit a short walk from King Street, so the bar scene is right there once you turn 21.

The Stats: College of Charleston (CofC)

Check out the College of Charleston gender ratio and the official Fraternity and Sorority Life page. See more on College of Charleston campus life. MUSC, The Citadel and Trident Tech students all end up on the same few blocks of King Street on the weekend.

21+ only

6 Bars for Anyone 21+ in Charleston

The College of Charleston bars everyone ends up at, most within walking distance of campus.

Under 21? You may find a posh.vip event at some of these places. Planning a whole night? Our King Street bar crawl route strings them together.

  • 1

    Uptown Social

    Sprawling multi-level venue with a massive rooftop, dance floor and multiple bars. The go-to for Thursday, Friday and Saturday nights.

  • 2

    Republic Garden & Lounge

    Club-like atmosphere with DJs, cocktails and a sophisticated vibe on King Street.

  • 3

    Trio Nightclub

    Charleston's electronic music destination with big sound and lighting, the closest thing to a Miami or Vegas-style club here.

  • 4

    Prohibition

    Craft cocktail bar with a speakeasy vibe, a step up from the typical college bar.

  • 5

    Ritual Rooftop Lounge

    Upscale rooftop with bottle service, VIP tables and a dress code. More Charleston rooftop bars here.

  • 6

    Live OnAir Club

    A multi-story honky tonk bar, newer to the Charleston scene.

💸 On a student budget: Burns Alley is a no-cover sports bar near campus with cheap drinks and live music. The Royal American on Morrison Drive has cheap beers, an outdoor deck and regular shows.
🚕 Play it safe: Most of these bars are walking distance on King Street. Start early, bar hop, and always have a safe ride home planned. Charleston bars close at 2 am, so line up your ride before last call, and grab late-night food on the way home.
