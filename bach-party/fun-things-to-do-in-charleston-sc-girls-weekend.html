Fun Things To Do in Charleston SC on a Girls Weekend

A Friday-to-Sunday plan you can mix and match. Pick one or two from each block and you have a full Charleston girls weekend.

Friday Night

Arrive, Settle In, Start the Party

🧺

Have the Rental Ready

Have Bienvenue Charleston stock the fridge and decorate before you land. Bringing your own decor? Pack giant letter balloons.

🍸

Cocktail Class at Home

A bartender from Nightcap Social Co. teaches the group three cocktails at your rental.

🃏

Game Night

Low-key first night? Split into teams for Midnight Taboo and order in.

⭐

The Surprise Show

End night one with Michael's private show at the rental. $200 for up to 90 minutes.

Saturday Morning

Brunch & King Street Shopping

⛪

Brunch in a Church

60-foot ceilings and stained glass at Church and Union, a block from the City Market.

🥞

Classic Southern Brunch

Shrimp and grits at Poogan's Porch, diner vibes at Miller's All Day, or a biscuit run to Callie's Hot Little Biscuit.

🎤

A Brunch Show

Make brunch the event with the Charleston brunch show.

🛍️

Shop Upper King

Matching outfits at Two Cumberland, or book the Tiny Tassel after hours for the group.

Saturday Afternoon

Boats, Beaches & Picnics

🛥️

BYOB Party Boat

Karaoke and beer pong on the harbor with Charleston Party Cat (site).

⛵

Sunset Sail

A classier harbor cruise on the tall ship Schooner Pride, or see all Charleston Harbor Tours.

🐬

Kayak With Dolphins

A guided Shem Creek kayak tour with Coastal Expeditions. No experience needed.

🧺

Luxury Picnic

A styled setup on the beach or at the rental from Holy City Picnic (site).

Any Afternoon

Classes, Workshops & Shows

🥂

Crafting & Mimosas

Pick from 20+ crafts with bottomless drinks at Craft-DIY on King Street (site).

🤠

Custom Hat Bar

Matching felt hats at Southern Ruetz (site), drink included.

🎨

Sip & Paint or Candles

Charleston Paint Party for sip and paint, or a candle-making class at Candlefish.

🔐

Escape Room

A private 60-minute game at Break Out Charleston (site).

😂

Improv Comedy

Cheap, fast and hilarious at Theatre 99.

🔎

Murder Mystery Dinner

Solve a case over dinner with The Dinner Detective.

🎮

Live Game Show

Compete on stage at The Epic Game Show Experience.

🪓

Axe Throwing & Games

Competitive fun with Charleston Activities.

Saturday Night

Dinner, Rooftops & a Night Out

🌇

Rooftop Drinks

Sunset at The Vendue, Stars or Citrus Club. See our 7 best rooftop bars near King Street.

🍻

Guided Pub Crawl

The bachelorette pub crawl, a themed crawl with Revelry Tours, or the haunted pub crawl. All 21+.

🪩

Live Music & Dancing

Funk at The Commodore, shows at Music Farm, drag at Dudley's.

🍾

VIP Table

Bottle service at Republic Garden & Lounge, or the party at Uptown Social. Then late-night food.

Planning a whole night out? Read the Charleston girls night out guide and the King Street bar crawl guide.

Sunday

Slow Morning, Last Bites

💆

Spa Morning

Group treatments at The Spa at Charleston Place.

🍤

Walking Food Tour

Lunch and sightseeing in one with Charleston Culinary Tours.

🥃

Distillery Tour

A $10 tour and whiskey flight at High Wire Distilling.

🎸

Free Music Before the Drive

Catch a free deck show at the Pour House on James Island.
