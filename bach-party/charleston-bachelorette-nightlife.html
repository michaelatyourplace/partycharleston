Charleston Bachelorette Nightlife

Updated 2026|5 min read

Charleston's night out runs along King Street, with a second cluster of lounges on Calhoun Street a short walk away. Most groups pick one dance floor, one dressy drinks stop, and one sing-along spot, then let the night take over from there. Every venue on this page is 21+.

  • Bars & Clubs
  • Planning The Night
  • Start At The Rental

Bars, Clubs & Party Spots

From rooftop bottle service to dive-bar pool tables, these are the spots we send bachelorette groups to. All venues listed below are 21+.

  • Ritual

    Tulum-inspired rooftop lounge on Calhoun Street with DJs, bottle service, and skyline views.

  • Republic Reign

    An indoor-outdoor garden lounge on King Street with live DJs, VIP tables, and a laid-back patio bar. Bachelorette night at Republic

  • Trio

    Charleston's longtime EDM nightclub — live bands downstairs, DJs and a dance floor upstairs.

  • Thoroughbred Club

    A classic hotel bar inside Charleston Place with nightly live music and craft cocktails.

  • The Bangkok Lounge

    Seven-nights-a-week karaoke bar on King Street with frozen drinks and a fun, no-judgment crowd.

  • John King Grill & Dueling Piano Bar

    Smash burgers by day, dueling pianos Wednesday–Saturday — a rowdy, sing-along night out.

  • Camellias

    Hotel Bennett's glamorous champagne bar — a dressy, Instagram-ready stop for the group.

  • Bourbon And Bubbles

    A chic King Street cocktail bar known for its bourbon list, champagne, and elevated shared plates.

  • Babylon

    A global-beats lounge on Calhoun Street with salsa, bachata, and bottle service.

  • Prohibition

    A 1920s-style bar and restaurant with live jazz, swing, and DJs on weekend nights.

  • On Air

    A three-floor King Street venue — Nashville-style honky-tonk downstairs, house music and a rooftop up top.

  • Last Saint

    An eastside cocktail bar with a moody front room and a quieter back room for conversation.

  • A.C.'s Bar & Grill

    A no-frills downtown dive bar — cheap drinks, pool tables, and a solid late-night burger.

  • The Commodore

    A retro, disco-ball dance bar on King Street built for a late night on the dance floor. Dancing at The Commodore

All nightlife bars & clubs listed above are 21+.

Planning The Night Out

Reserve VIP tables at Ritual, Babylon, or Republic Reign a week or two ahead on spring and fall weekends, and bring a sash so the door staff knows who the bride is. King Street is walkable from end to end, so most groups only need a ride there and back. Our Charleston bachelorette transportation guide covers rideshares and party buses, and the King Street bar crawl, rooftop bars, and late-night food guides fill in the rest of the route.

Start The Night At The Rental

The best bachelorette nights in Charleston start before anyone calls a ride. Michael At Your Place brings a private bachelorette show to your Airbnb or rental house across Charleston, Mount Pleasant, and the beaches, so the crew gets the party going over drinks at home before heading downtown. The show runs up to 90 minutes, is as interactive or as hands-off as the group wants, and last-minute dates are welcome. Book it for 8 or 9 p.m., then hit King Street right after. Text or call 843-360-9438, or see the Michael At Your Place show details.
