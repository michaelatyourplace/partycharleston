Church and Union Bachelorette Brunch & Dinner

A group meal inside a converted century-old church, a block from the City Market.

Why it works

The dining room has 60-foot ceilings and the original stained glass, which makes for some of the best group photos in town. Do weekend brunch to kick off the day, or dinner before a night out on East Bay; it stays open until 2 am Thursday through Saturday. Bigger crews can ask about the private room.
