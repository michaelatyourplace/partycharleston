Giant BRIDE Balloons for Your Bachelorette Airbnb

Big white BRIDE letters that turn any Charleston rental into the photo spot of the weekend.

Why it works

Hang them over the bed, in the living room or behind the bar cart and you have your backdrop for welcome photos and the night-out group shot. They pack flat in a suitcase, so you can set them up the night you arrive without hiring a decorator. Pair them with a few more of our Charleston bachelorette ideas for the rest of the weekend.

What's included

  • BRIDE foil letter balloons, 40 inches
  • 32 ft white ribbon roll
  • Straw for inflating

Setup tips

  • Inflate with the straw and hang with the ribbon or removable wall hooks
  • Helium is optional; most Airbnbs look best with letters taped to a wall
  • Use removable hooks, not tape, on painted walls to protect your deposit
