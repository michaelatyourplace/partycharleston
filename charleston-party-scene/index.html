Downtown Charleston: The Undisputed Party Hub

When you think of a "party" in the Lowcountry, one place comes to mind: Downtown Charleston. The historic peninsula is the center of the nightlife, and North Charleston and the islands orbit around it. Upper King Street is where most nights start, so begin with the King Street bar crawl.

Venues like Republic Garden & Lounge, Trio and Stars Rooftop & Grill Room are household names for anyone looking for a bar. For a quieter start, catch sunset at one of the Charleston rooftop bars, and finish with late-night food. Under 21? Use the 18+ nightlife list instead.

Activities At Night In Charleston SC

The Charleston party scene is a fascinating ecosystem, and downtown is the hub in every sense: the density, the variety and the energy all live on a few blocks.
