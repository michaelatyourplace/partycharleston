Charleston Nightlife Under 21

The best of Charleston after dark for ages 18 to 20: live music, 18+ shows, late-night eats, and social spots across the Holy City. The list of 18+ clubs in Charleston is short, so it pays to know where to look.

Night clubs in Charleston, SC for 18 and over are mostly event nights and late-night spots that let in a younger crowd. Plan the night around shows, local hangouts, and outdoor events instead of bars.

Late-Night Dining Spots To Hang

Charleston Bars 18+

Most bars on King Street card for 21+ at the door at night, so the move for the 18 to 20 crowd is restaurants with late kitchens. Spots that stay open late work as hangouts the same way 18+ clubs do, and they're a relaxed setting for groups 18 and over. Kickin' Chicken and Carmella's Café offer sweet and savory menus that work well after a show.

18 and Up Things To Do Before Dark

Half the fun in Charleston happens in daylight, and none of it needs a bar.

🌊

Folly Beach Day

The classic student beach, about 20 minutes from downtown. Go early on weekends before parking fills. More in our college guide.

🔐

Escape Room

A private 60-minute game for your group at Break Out Charleston in West Ashley.

🐬

Kayak With Dolphins

A guided Shem Creek kayak tour in Mount Pleasant. No experience needed.

🧺

Picnic Pop-Up

A styled luxury picnic, set up and cleared away for you. Great for birthdays.

🍤

Food Tour

A walking food tour through the French Quarter that doubles as lunch.

🛍️

Shop King Street

Boutiques all along King Street, like Two Cumberland for a going-out fit.
