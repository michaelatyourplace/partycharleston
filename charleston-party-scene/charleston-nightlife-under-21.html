Charleston Nightlife Under 21

The best of Charleston after dark for ages 18 to 20: live music, 18+ shows, late-night eats, and social spots across the Holy City. The list of 18+ clubs in Charleston is short, so it pays to know where to look.

Night clubs in Charleston, SC for 18 and over are mostly event nights and late-night spots that let in a younger crowd. Plan the night around shows, local hangouts, and outdoor events instead of bars.

Late-Night Dining Spots To Hang

Charleston Bars 18+

Spots that stay open late can work as hangouts the same way 18+ clubs do. Many Charleston restaurants keep their kitchens open late, which makes for a relaxed setting for groups 18 and over. Fuel, Kickin' Chicken, and Carmella's Café offer sweet and savory menus that work well after a show.
