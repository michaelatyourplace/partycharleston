The Late-Night Food Guide: Best Eats on King Street After 1 AM

It's 1:45 AM. The lights just came on at Uptown and thousands of people are pouring onto King Street. Your feet hurt and you are starving. This is the most critical moment of your night.

🍕 Pizza 🍔 Bar food 🥤 Cook Out run ✅ The plan

The wrong choice means a 45-minute wait for a mediocre slice. The right one is the perfect end to an epic night. Charleston bars close at 2 AM, so plan the food stop before the rush. When it's that late you need one of three things: pizza, a real meal, or something greasy.

The Pizza Gods

Benny's & Paolo's

Upper King

Benny Ravello's

The king of the 2 AM exodus from Upper King. Benny's serves gigantic, New York-style slices, and the line stretches down the block. Don't be fooled: it's built for volume and moves fast. Be in and out in about 10 minutes with a massive, foldable slice. A Charleston rite of passage.

OrderOne giant slice
WaitLong line, fast
VibePost-bar chaos
Market Street

Paolo's Gelato & Pizza

The go-to for the Mid and Lower King crowd. A classic, no-frills slice joint that's served the late-night crowd for years, with gelato if you'd rather go sweet. A more old-school Charleston feel than Benny's.

OrderSlice + gelato
WaitShorter
VibeOld-school
The Pro Move · 21+

The Royal American

While the college crowd swarms Benny's, the local industry crowd and CofC students in the know head to The Royal American on Morrison Drive, a short ride from Upper King. The kitchen stays open late, and the bar food is unbelievably good: loaded tater tots, burgers and fried chicken sandwiches. It's a bar, so you have to be 21+, but it's the perfect place to wind down with real food instead of grabbing it on the go.

OrderTots + a burger
Ages21+
VibeLocal dive
The Real-Meal Mission

Cook Out

Sometimes you don't want a slice. You want a burger, fries and a milkshake tray for under $10. The Cook Out on Savannah Highway, a short Uber from downtown, is the mecca for this. Piling into an Uber for a Cook Out run is a sacred post-party ritual.

OrderThe tray
Getting thereShort Uber
VibeDrive-thru glory

The Verdict: Your Late-Night Food Plan

The Classic Move

Join the crowd at Benny Ravello's. It's part of the experience.

The 21+ Wind-Down

A final drink and real food at The Royal American.

The Mission Move

Commit to the Uber for a glorious Cook Out tray.

Your food choice says a lot about your King Street bar crawl strategy. Whatever you pick, pick something. Your head will thank you in the morning. Under 21? Try the dessert crawl instead.
