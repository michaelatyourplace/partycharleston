Charleston Pub Crawl Tours

Want someone else to plan the route? These local companies run guided bar crawls downtown. All are 21+ with a valid ID; check current times and prices when you book.

Bachelorette favoriteFrench Quarter

The Original Charleston Pub & Brewery Tour

A walking crawl through about 10 blocks of the oldest part of the city, with a dedicated bachelorette version and group tours for up to 50.

LengthAbout 2.5 hours
Stops3–5 historic pubs
Good to knowDrinks bought at each stop
Themed crawlsNightly at 9 PM

Revelry Tours

Three story-driven crawls: Bay Street Bastards (true crime), Speakeasy Sagas (Prohibition) and 'Til Death Do Us Part, which they pitch to bachelorettes.

Starts9 PM nightly
Ages21+
Good to knowPick the theme that fits
Ghost storiesCity Market

Nightly Spirits Haunted Pub Crawl

Haunted history at old downtown bars, leaving from Henry's on the Market. Runs most evenings, rain or shine.

LengthAbout 2.5 hours
Meet54 N Market St
Good to knowDrinks not included

Cocktail Tours & Self-Guided Crawls

Prefer cocktails to pubs? Try the Upper King Street cocktail tasting tour. Want to go at your own pace? There's an app-based Charleston bar crawl scavenger hunt.

Build Your Own King Street Crawl

Rather do it yourself? Start with sunset at the rooftop bars, grab a VIP table at Republic, dance to live funk at The Commodore, and finish with late-night food. Bars close at 2 AM, so start early.

Before Dark

High Wire distillery tour or crafting and mimosas on King.

Dinner

Church and Union or a walking food tour.

Off the Strip

Free deck shows at the Pour House on James Island.

Under 21 on King Street

The pub crawls above are 21+, but King Street still works: shop Upper King, book an escape room, then do a dessert crawl. The full list of 18+ clubs and shows is in our Charleston nightlife under 21 guide, and students can see the college nightlife guide.
