The 7 Best Rooftop Bars Near King Street

In a city this beautiful, drinking at street level is a missed opportunity. Charleston's protected skyline of church steeples makes its rooftop bars some of the most charming in the country.

🌇 The top 7 🗺️ Rooftop crawls

Not all rooftops are equal. Some are high-energy party scenes, others are quiet, ultra-luxe lounges. Here are the best in and around King Street, ranked for your King Street bar crawl, whether it's a classy start, a ladies night out or a wild night.

The Rooftop Rundown: Our Top 7 Picks

1
The Classic, Can't-Miss ViewLower King · East Bay

The Vendue (The Rooftop Bar)

Consistently voted a top rooftop by locals, The Vendue sits just off East Bay, a quick walk from Lower King. It has the best panoramic view in Charleston: the harbor, the Ravenel Bridge, Waterfront Park and the whole historic skyline. It's a big two-level space with multiple bars and a mix of tourists and locals. Packed, but upscale-social rather than wild.

Best forFirst-timers, dates, bachelorette kick-offs
Drink thisFrozen cocktails or rosé
Pro tipGet there before sunset. Seating is first-come and fills fast, and evenings may be 21+.
2
The 360° View & Classic FeelMid-King

Stars Rooftop & Grill Room

A Charleston classic on Mid-King. The Grill Room downstairs is a nice restaurant, but the prize is the 360-degree rooftop, one of the few spots where you can see all of King Street from above. Wood-paneled and more grown-up, it's a great pre-dinner sunset drink or a calmer alternative to Upper King.

Best forSunset drinks, starting a classy night
Drink thisLocal craft beer or an Old Fashioned
Pro tipThe rooftop menu is limited, so eat downstairs. Check the evening age policy.
3
The See-and-Be-Seen Club SceneUpper King · Moxy hotel

Ritual Rooftop Restaurant & Lounge

If The Vendue is classy, Ritual is a party. Up top at the Moxy on Upper King, it's a big, Tulum-inspired space with DJs, a dance floor, VIP sections and a club atmosphere. You're here for the scene, the music and the energy.

Best forLate-night dancing, large groups, bottle service
Drink thisSignature cocktails or bottle service
Pro tipExpect a line and a dress code on weekends. It gets going after 10 PM.
4
The College Party RooftopUpper King

Uptown Social

Known as a massive sports bar and party spot, Uptown's rooftop is one of the biggest on Upper King. It's not a sit-and-sip spot. It's an open-air extension of the party downstairs, packed with the 21+ college crowd. Big group, high energy, house-party-on-a-roof: this is it.

Best forThe college crowd, game days, big groups
Drink thisA frozen drink or a bucket of beers
Pro tipOne of the most casual rooftops. No need to dress up.
5
The Ultra-Luxe EscapeMarion Square

Fiat Lux at Hotel Bennett

The most luxurious rooftop on the list, in the Hotel Bennett overlooking Marion Square. It's a quiet, chic (and pricey) cocktail bar with a gorgeous central bar and fireplace seating. You're not partying here. You're sipping a craft cocktail and feeling like royalty.

Best forSpecial occasions, a romantic drink
Drink thisA classic Martini or real champagne
Pro tipDress the part, and reserve ahead.
6
The Artsy Garden PartyLower King · Market & King

Élevé at the Grand Bohemian

Atop the Grand Bohemian Hotel near Market and King, Élevé is a whimsical, art-filled space that feels like a hidden garden. More restaurant than bar, the terrace is perfect for a glass of wine and a long conversation, and an easy Lower King starting point.

Best forA creative date, a bachelorette brunch
Drink thisSparkling wine or a floral cocktail
Pro tipBest for brunch or an early-evening drink.
7
The Mid-Century HotspotMarion Square

Citrus Club at The Dewberry

Up top at The Dewberry, Citrus Club has some of the highest views in the city, looking over Marion Square. Mid-century modern decor and bright, citrus-themed cocktails give it a private-club feel, and it's notoriously hard to get into.

Best forA trendy crowd, cocktail lovers
Drink thisThe signature citrus cocktails
Pro tipReserve well ahead. Hotel guests get priority.

The Verdict: How to Plan Your Rooftop Crawl

You can build a whole King Street bar crawl just by hopping between rooftops. Pick your mood:

The Classy Crawl

Start at Fiat Lux for an ultra-luxe drink, move to Élevé for the artsy vibe, and end at The Vendue for the sunset harbor view. Pricey, but unforgettable.

The Party Crawl

Start at Stars for the 360° sunset, head to Uptown Social for the high-energy party, then dance until close at Ritual. Want a VIP table instead? Book one at Republic Garden & Lounge.

Either way, you'll see the Holy City the way most people miss it. When the night ends, here's the best late-night food on King Street.
